# Authorship and CRediT checklist

- [ ] Identify every human who satisfies the target venue's authorship policy.
- [ ] Confirm that every author can defend the scientific claims.
- [ ] Confirm contributor consent and ordering.
- [ ] Assign CRediT roles based on actual human contributions.
- [ ] Confirm affiliations and contact details.
- [ ] Confirm funding and conflicts.
- [ ] Exclude AI systems from authorship and CRediT roles.
- [ ] Preserve the substantive AI-use disclosure.

Names, roles, approvals, and date: intentionally blank.
