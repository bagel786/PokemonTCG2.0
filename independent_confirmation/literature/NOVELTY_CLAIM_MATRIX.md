## Verdict

No central contribution survives the kill conditions. The exact class labels may be pedagogically useful, and a correctly implemented tool may be useful engineering, but neither establishes manuscript-level novelty under the takeover brief's standard.

The benchmark is especially vulnerable to circularity: the router is a deterministic encoding of the evidence contract, while existing G01-G19 truth labels and planned synthetic replacements are generated from that same contract. Role-separated AI construction changes who types the fixtures, not the epistemic source of truth.

The permitted narrow reframe was considered and rejected for the current campaign. A future project would need a corpus sourced independently of the router, blind human or external adjudication for non-mechanical labels, and a contribution that is more than applying the Eckmann/Sabot/ASMR/MLE/entrapment pattern to stochastic experiments.
