# Role of the Historical Restricted-Engine Case

**Status (frozen):** supplementary/motivating ONLY. Not evidence for the redesigned framework's validity.

## Required disclosures whenever the case appears in any manuscript

1. **Restricted materials:** the engine, traces, and opponent data cannot be redistributed; results are reported at summary level only and cannot be independently recomputed from released artifacts.
2. **No event alignment:** the engine exposes no semantic event identifiers; Branch E claims were impossible there.
3. **Factorial candidate traces absent:** candidate execution traces were not retained for all arms; several counterfactual questions are permanently unanswerable for that system.
4. **Post-acquisition taxonomy:** the claim taxonomy was constructed AFTER the historical failures were observed; the case is a motivation, not a validation.
5. **Retrospective suppression:** the historical suppression decision was made retrospectively under an evolving rule; it is reported as engineering history, not as a prospective measurement of false-suppression behavior.
6. **Not validation:** nothing about the redesigned framework's detection/false-suppression performance follows from this case.

## Numbers that may be cited, with required framing

- 3,000/3,000 deterministic preflight executions with zero required mismatches — *engineering preflight on one restricted build*.
- 99/200 timed-search seed-condition clusters disagreeing on the declared trace projection — *one battery, one build, retrospective diagnosis*.
- 210/2,800 outcome-record and 458/2,800 available-record disagreements in one suppressed comparison; later 458/2,800 under a frozen narrower rule — *historical bookkeeping under evolving rules; never pooled with prospective open-system results*.

## Placement rules

May appear: Introduction (≤1 paragraph motivation), Section "Restricted historical case" (supplementary), Appendix.
Must NOT appear: abstract results sentences alongside open-system numbers, cross-system consistency claims, or generalization statements.
