prospective protocol documentation, benchmark and analysis code, diagnostic
repairs, figure/table generation, manuscript drafting, release assembly, and
integrity auditing. Exact deployed model snapshots were not exposed by the
interface. No AI system is an author, and AI-generated text or code was not
treated as scientific evidence.

The human supplied the scientific redesign specification and retained authority
over scope, restricted materials, authorship, scientific interpretation, and any
future submission. Verification included a public git-history freeze before
final outcomes, raw-row hashing and schema checks, independent reaggregation,
equation fixtures, machine-readable figure/table sources, contradiction and
reference audits, actual installed-license inspection, and PDF render review.
Outputs that could not be verified from retained rows were rejected or labeled
diagnostic-only. The detailed accepted/rejected task record appears in
`AI_USE_LOG.csv`.

The AI had access to the local repository, including bounded historical
aggregate descriptions of a restricted engine case. Restricted engine assets,
opponent packages, and legacy raw traces are not included in the public release
candidate. No human-subject or private user data were used in the prospective
benchmark.

No substantive use of Ox Alpha, DeepSeek, or another non-OpenAI model is
evidenced in the redesign artifacts audited here. The human author must amend
this disclosure and log before submission if such systems were used outside the
recorded workflow.

Venue-specific wording and placement require human confirmation at submission
time.
