# APS Open Science submission strategy

Checked against APS pages on 2026-08-24.

## Recommended article type and positioning

Submit as a **Regular Article**, not as a Protocol article. APS Open Science
defines Regular Articles as original research articles with no length limit.
Protocol articles are for procedures that have already been proven effective;
this manuscript instead proposes PEVL, validates known failure modes in an open
testbed, and evaluates it in one restricted-engine case study.

The fit is direct but should be made explicit in the cover letter. The journal's
scope includes artificial intelligence, computational and data-intensive
science, technical and methodological advances, software advances,
benchmarking, and negative or null findings. The manuscript should lead with
methodological usefulness and fail-closed evidence, not with competitive game
performance.

Sources:

- [APS Open Science information for authors](https://journals.aps.org/apsos/authors)
- [APS Open Science scope](https://journals.aps.org/apsos/scope)
- [About APS Open Science](https://journals.aps.org/apsos/about)

## Submission-critical requirements

- Submit the compiled REVTeX PDF as a Regular Article.
- Include a Data Availability Statement in the manuscript and submission form.
  Public data and software should receive an archival citation; GitHub alone is
  useful for review but is not the final FAIR archive.
- Keep restricted engine, opponent, card, and deck material out of the public
  release unless the corresponding author confirms redistribution rights.
- Include substantive AI assistance in the Methods or Acknowledgments as
  applicable. APS requires the tool name and version, how it assisted, and how
  authors directed and verified the output. Human authors retain full
  responsibility.
- Supply final author names, affiliations, contact-author email, author
  contributions, conflicts, and corresponding-author ORCID before submission.
- Confirm that references are current and that every preprint is labeled as
  such rather than described as settled peer-reviewed evidence.

Sources:

- [APS policy on appropriate use of AI tools](https://journals.aps.org/authors/appropriate-use-ai-tools)
- [APS editorial policies and practices](https://journals.aps.org/authors/editorial-policies)

## Time-sensitive publishing note

The APS Open Science home page currently states that the article-processing
charge is waived for submissions and transfers received **before 2026-09-01**.
This is a submission incentive, not permission to bypass the scientific or
rights checks. The remaining human blockers should be resolved early enough to
allow the authors to review the final PDF and submit by 2026-08-31 if they wish
to use the waiver.

Source: [APS Open Science home page](https://journals.aps.org/apsos/)

## Final human decisions

Before journal submission, the corresponding author must:

1. approve the author list and contribution statement;
2. approve the rights/license position and public-release contents;
3. create or approve the archival repository record and DOI;
4. verify the AI disclosure against the exact tools used across the project;
5. review every scientific claim and the final rendered PDF; and
6. perform the submission itself or explicitly authorize that external action.
