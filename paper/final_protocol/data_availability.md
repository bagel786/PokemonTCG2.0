# Data Availability Statement

The companion **review package** included in this repository contains the
self-contained synthetic protocol implementation and fixtures, expected
outputs, processed historical and prospective diagnostics, sufficient
processed factorial rows for the included reaggregation, analysis and artifact
code, frozen protocols, generated figures and source data, tests, dependency
information, manifests, notices, citation metadata, and exact reproduction
commands. It is designed to reproduce the synthetic and processed analyses
without the restricted game engine. Cryptographic identities bind the processed
evidence to the audited local sources.

These included components are technical candidates for a public companion
artifact, but **public archival availability and redistribution rights are not
established**. The human author has not confirmed ownership, redistribution
authority, an open-source/software-data license, archive creators, maintainer
contact, or a persistent DOI. The current no-license status grants no permission
to copy, modify, or redistribute the package. This statement therefore does not
describe the package as public, promise a future release, or cite an uncreated
archive.

The following restricted case-study materials are not included: the tournament
engine and source, engine binaries, third-party opponent packages, game assets
and metadata, private replay observations, policy packages and model weights,
and raw restricted traces. Organizer terms, third-party rights, privacy
constraints, and unresolved release authority prevent the repository from
granting access. The package retains only neutralized processed summaries,
approved digests, and bounded source-data exports. Those materials are not
offered “on request” because the repository establishes neither legal authority
nor a practical controlled-access mechanism.

Consequently, readers can inspect and regenerate the synthetic fixtures,
reaggregate the included processed diagnostics and factorial outcomes, rebuild
tables and figures, and verify the admission logic. They cannot independently
replay restricted gameplay end to end. Exact restricted-trajectory regeneration
would require separately authorized engine and package access that this article
cannot provide.

A software/data reference and DOI may be added only after the human author
approves ownership, license, archive creation, creators, version, and maintainer
metadata and after the persistent record actually exists. Until then, DOI and
license fields must remain explicit blockers rather than placeholders that
could be mistaken for valid identifiers.
