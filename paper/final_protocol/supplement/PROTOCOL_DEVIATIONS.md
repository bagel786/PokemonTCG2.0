## Frozen case-study plan

For the retained deterministic preflight, timed-search stress test, and
five-context factorial acquisitions, Git places the frozen protocol before the
result artifacts. The plan used binary consequences:

- one required preflight mismatch blocked factorial acquisition;
- one invalidating acquisition, schedule, error, or repeated-C1 mismatch
  suppressed every planned factorial contrast; and
- one stress-test trace-digest mismatch rejected exact reproducibility for the
  exercised opponent/condition while retaining the result.

Conditional on the factorial gates passing, the plan described the
finite-population seed-matched estimates with 100,000 paired bootstrap resamples
within the ten strata and 95% intervals. It also specified secondary exact
two-sided McNemar inference for the primary binary C4-minus-C1 contrast.

The historical repeated-control audit is not covered by the same prospective
chronology. Its outcomes already existed when the available-record gate and
suppression consequence were frozen. Applying that retrospectively frozen rule
without dropping mismatches is auditable, but it is not prospective blinding
evidence.

## D01 — Post-acquisition taxonomy and descriptive relabeling

- **Frozen plan:** Binary acquisition/suppression gates followed, on a pass, by
  finite-population paired bootstrap intervals and secondary exact McNemar
  inference.
- **Later treatment:** A multi-level generic admission taxonomy and executable
  rule bundle were formalized after acquisition. The article conservatively
  relabels the paired-bootstrap output as fixed-battery empirical reweighting
  quantiles and retains the McNemar value only for numerical audit, not admitted
  inference.
- **Effect on evidence:** This narrows the final claims and does not alter the
  underlying contrast or resampling arithmetic. It cannot retroactively show
  that the later taxonomy was prospectively frozen or validated.
- **Future requirement:** A future prospective study must freeze the taxonomy,
  evidence inputs, estimands, analysis labels, and reporting consequences before
  acquisition.
- **Status:** Disclosure added; human confirmation **PENDING**. No retrospective
  signoff or amendment is asserted.

## D02 — Stress-endpoint integrity hardening

- **Frozen endpoint:** Equality of the four complete public-state/action trace
  digests was the primary endpoint.
- **Later hardening:** The article's executable definition compares the ordered
  `(digest, byte_count)` tuple, so a byte-count-only difference also fails.
- **Observed effect:** None on the primary count. Digest disagreement is 99/200;
  digest-or-byte disagreement is also 99/200; the 96 byte-count disagreement
  clusters are contained within the 99 digest-disagreement clusters.
- **Interpretation:** Byte count is a post-acquisition integrity check, not a
  prospectively frozen replacement endpoint and not evidence of stronger
  prospective validation.
- **Status:** Provenance disclosed; human confirmation **PENDING**.
