# Rights and access audit

Status: **HUMAN/LEGAL CONFIRMATION REQUIRED — PUBLIC REDISTRIBUTION NOT
AUTHORIZED BY THIS AUDIT.**

This audit records the authority established by the repository; it is not legal
advice and does not infer ownership from Git history. Competition and
third-party notices reviewed in the source tree restrict the game engine and
related materials. No repository-level record establishes that the paper
authors may redistribute all newly authored code, processed engine-derived
records, policy packages, or third-party materials. The companion directory is
therefore a review package with a no-license notice, not a public deposit.

## Excluded material

- tournament-engine source and binaries;
- third-party opponent packages;
- game assets, card data, rules metadata, and private replay observations;
- raw restricted traces and observations;
- policy packages, model weights, and deployable agents;
- credentials, participant identifiers, local absolute paths, and unreviewed
  archives.

## Included review-package material

- a self-contained synthetic conformance implementation, fixtures, schema, and
  expected outputs;
- neutralized processed historical, preflight, timed-search, and factorial
  diagnostics sufficient for the included reaggregation;
- protocols, analysis and artifact code, tests, figures, source data, manifests,
  and citation placeholders.

Included material is a technical candidate for a later human-authorized
deposit; inclusion here does not itself grant redistribution rights.

## Blocking human actions

1. Identify every author and contributor and determine ownership of each
   release file.
2. Obtain qualified review of competition terms, continued possession, derived
   records, privacy, and third-party rights.
3. Confirm which code and processed data may be published and select approved
   licenses; otherwise preserve an explicit access restriction.
4. Confirm that neutralization and aggregate disclosure do not expose protected
   participant or package information.
5. Approve creators, maintainer/contact, version, repository metadata, and any
   archive deposit before adding a DOI.

Until these actions are complete, the manuscript must say that public archival
availability is not established, must not offer restricted material on request,
and must not promise a future release.
