# APS Submission Questionnaire

Copy this whole document into Google Docs, answer the 20 questions in plain
English (a sentence is enough), and send the completed file back. Do not worry
about formatting or exact wording — the tooling turns your answers into the
formal APS disclosures, licenses, and metadata. Nothing is submitted or
published until you approve the final PDF.

---

## A. AUTHORS

**1. Who should be listed as authors, in order?**
(Exact names as they should appear in the byline.)

**2. Who is the corresponding author?**
(The one person who handles correspondence with APS.)

**3. Did anyone else contribute substantially enough that they may deserve
authorship? Briefly explain what they did.**
(If none, write "no one else".)

## B. CONTACT / AFFILIATION

**4. What affiliation should appear?**
Examples:
- Leander High School, Leander, Texas, USA
- Independent Researcher, Leander, Texas, USA
- another accurate affiliation

**5. Corresponding email?**
(This will appear publicly on the paper.)

**6. ORCID iD, if any?**
(16 digits like 0000-0000-0000-0000; APS requires one for the corresponding
author. Write "none" only if you will register one later — APS will ask.)

## C. WHO DID WHAT

**7. In plain English, what did each author contribute?**
One or two sentences per person is fine (e.g., "I designed the study, wrote
all the code, ran the experiments, and wrote the paper"). The tooling will
translate this into the formal contribution categories and show you the
suggestion for approval — you do not need to fill any table.

## D. FUNDING / CONFLICTS / ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

**8. Was this research externally funded? If yes, by whom?**
(If no, write "no external funding".)

**9. Is there anything that could reasonably look like a financial or
nonfinancial conflict of interest?**
(Examples: employment by or contract with the game's publisher or tournament
organizer; paid access to the platform; a close personal relationship with
rights holders. If none, write "none".)

**10. Anyone or any institution you would like to thank in the
acknowledgments?**
(Only name people who are OK with being named. If none, write "none".)

## E. PRIOR PUBLIC MATERIAL

**11. Was any version, report, or discussion of this work publicly posted
before?**
(For example a Kaggle competition write-up or forum thread, a blog post, a
preprint, or a prior submission elsewhere. Give titles/links if you know
them; if none, write "none".)

## F. AI DISCLOSURE

**12. Besides OpenAI Codex, what AI tools were used substantively?**
For each tool, just give: the tool name, roughly what it was used for, and
whether any private or restricted material was uploaded to it.
(If none besides Codex, write "none".)

## G. PUBLIC CODE / DATA RELEASE

**13. Do you confirm that the human-authored, sanitized code and processed
results may be publicly released, while the restricted game engine, opponent
packages, and game materials stay excluded?**
(YES / NO — if NO, the paper ships with a "not publicly available" statement
instead, which APS permits with reasons.)

**14. If yes to 13: are you comfortable with MIT for the code and CC BY 4.0
for the processed data/documents?**
(These are common default recommendations, not legal advice. Say "yes",
"yes but change to ...", or "advise me".)

**15. Do you authorize creating a Zenodo/OSF-style archival release and DOI
after you review the final release package?**
(YES / NOT YET — "not yet" simply means the paper describes the package as
available-on-completion; the DOI is added later.)

## H. MISSING SECONDARY OUTPUTS

**16. Confirm or correct this statement:**
"First-divergence actor-side counts and timing summaries were recovered.
First-divergence positions were never recorded. The missing positions do not
change the 99/200 primary trace-disagreement result."
(CONFIRM / CORRECT: ...)

**17. May the recovered actor-side counts and timing summaries be included in
the sanitized public release?**
(YES / NO — if NO they stay out of the public package and the paper says so.)

## I. SCIENTIFIC UNDERSTANDING

**18. After reading the short explanations below, do you personally
understand and agree with each? (If NO to any, list which.)**
- Same recorded seed ≠ same execution (timing/process differences can change
  runs).
- Repeatable execution ≠ event alignment (identical runs don't prove two
  different policies saw the same random events).
- 99/200 is a fixed-battery empirical result (a fact about these 200 tested
  conditions, not an estimate about all conditions).
- The later factorial did not show a resolved policy improvement (+0.55
  points with a range that spans zero).
- The generic claim taxonomy was formalized after the data existed.
- The historical suppression example was retrospective, not blinded.

**19. Are there any statements or numbers in the manuscript that you do not
understand, or do not feel comfortable taking responsibility for?**
(If none, write "none". This is your chance to flag anything before
sign-off.)

**20. Do you approve the final manuscript for submission once your answers
are incorporated and the final PDF is shown to you?**
