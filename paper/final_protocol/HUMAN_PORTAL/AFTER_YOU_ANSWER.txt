AFTER YOU ANSWER — AGENT WORKFLOW (not for the human)
=====================================================

When the human returns the completed questionnaire (Google Doc / plain text):

 1. Save the plain answers verbatim as:
        paper/final_protocol/HUMAN_RESPONSE.txt
 2. Run:
        python3 paper/final_protocol/scripts/parse_human_response.py
    It writes paper/final_protocol/human_answers.generated.yaml and lists any
    missing/ambiguous answers.
 3. Ask ONLY unavoidable clarifications (one concise question each; the parser
    prints them). Never invent an answer.
 4. Run:
        python3 paper/final_protocol/scripts/apply_human_response.py --diff
    Review the preview, then:
        python3 paper/final_protocol/scripts/apply_human_response.py --apply
    This updates the manuscript title block, author list, affiliation, email,
    ORCID, contribution statement (+ suggested contribution categories for
    approval), funding, competing interests, acknowledgments, prior-material
    disclosure, AI disclosure, deviation language, release authorization,
    license files, CITATION.cff, release status, archive metadata, Data
    Availability Statement, cover letter, and APS submission metadata.
 5. Build the licensed public-release candidate:
        python3 paper/final_protocol/scripts/build_public_release_candidate.py
 6. STOP for archive/DOI authorization if question 15 was "NOT YET".
    (If YES and a deposit has been made, insert the live DOI via the generated
    YAML `release_rights.doi`, then re-run step 4.)
 7. Rebuild the manuscript:
        SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=1787529600 tectonic paper/final_protocol/main.tex
 8. Re-run all audits:
        python3 paper/final_protocol/scripts/machine_finalize.py
    (covers raw hashes, reaggregation, statistics, synthetic suite, admission
    properties, tables/figures, claim ledger, novelty/reference audits,
    chronology + contradiction audits, release rebuild + manifest,
    clean-environment checks, REVTeX compile, all-page rendering, visual
    checks, submission bundle, final desk simulation)
 9. Render all pages and figures; confirm no overlapping text.
10. Generate the final APS submission bundle:
        python3 paper/final_protocol/scripts/build_submission_bundle.py
11. Output a ONE-PAGE final approval summary for the human containing:
    final title/byline, the eight passage dispositions, the core-facts
    confirmation, release/license choices, and the exact APS upload list.
12. The human inspects the final PDF and manually submits to APS.

Hard rules: never auto-approve authorship, licenses, release, or submission;
if an answer is ambiguous, stop and ask one concise question.
