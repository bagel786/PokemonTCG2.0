Dear Editors,

We seek consideration of “A Trace-Based Validation Protocol for Seed-Matched
Evaluations of Black-Box Game-Playing Agents” as an **APS Open Science Protocol
Article**.

Recording the same seed in two game-agent evaluations establishes a matched
schedule, but it does not establish that either execution repeats or that the
same semantic random events remain aligned after policy paths diverge. The
article presents an executable, fail-closed protocol that binds artifact and
observable engine-boundary seed identity, verifies row-level schedule parity,
compares an identical-arm recorded-trace projection across repeat and worker
contexts, audits stochastic sources, and applies a prospectively frozen rule
that admits, downgrades, or suppresses statistical claims.

The contribution is operational integration, not a claim to have discovered
common random numbers, streams and substreams, paired-seed evaluation,
identical-arm controls, event-keyed randomness, metamorphic testing, simulation
verification, or fixed-work search. A self-contained five-mode conformance
suite demonstrates the relevant non-implications and an event-keyed repair only
within its logged event ontology. The restricted game environment serves as a
case study rather than as a claim of competitive agent strength.

The case study shows why claim admission matters. A historical repeated-control
audit found disagreements in 210 of 2,800 available-outcome (win/draw) records
and 458 of 2,800 available records after decision count was added, so the frozen
historical factorial was suppressed. A prospectively frozen deterministic
preflight then found zero required mismatches across 1,000 arm–seed-condition
units and 3,000 executions. A separate timed-search diagnostic found 99 of 200
trace-projection disagreement clusters (49.5%; empirical 95% whole-cluster-
resampling interval 42.5%–56.5%). The admitted five-context factorial used
2,000 fixed-schedule units per cell; its total contrast was +0.55 percentage
points with an empirical 95% paired-resampling interval from −2.05 to +3.15
percentage points. This null-compatible result does not establish equivalence,
policy superiority, full common-random-number coupling, or generalization
beyond the frozen battery.

The review package is designed to reproduce the synthetic and processed
analyses without the restricted engine. It includes processed diagnostics,
sufficient processed factorial rows, protocols, analysis and artifact code,
tests, source data, and manifests. The tournament engine and source, engine
binaries, third-party opponent packages, game assets and metadata, private
replay observations, policy packages, and raw restricted traces are excluded
