# Competing-interests statement

**Submission blocker: human confirmation required.** The repository does not
contain a reliable, author-approved disclosure. Each author must disclose all
relevant financial and nonfinancial interests, including competition
affiliations, employment, consulting, sponsorship, grants, platform access,
intellectual-property interests, and relationships with the engine, game, data,
or rights holders.

The corresponding author must choose and approve exactly one final form after
collecting every author’s disclosure:

1. **No interests:** “The authors declare no competing interests.”
2. **Interests present:** **[describe each interest, affected author, and
   relationship in human-approved language].**

Neither form is currently established. Silence in Git history or project files
must not be interpreted as an absence of competing interests.
